# Gem & Glow — Diamond Painting Website

A responsive starter website for a diamond painting business. Built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript—no framework or build step needed.

## Files
- `index.html` — page structure, product cards, sections, cart panel
- `style.css` — responsive styling and mobile navigation
- `script.js` — product filters, add-to-bag, quantity controls, subtotal, demo newsletter form
- `README.md` — setup and customization notes

## Run it
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open `index.html` in a browser. For local development, you can also use VS Code's Live Server extension.
3. Internet is needed for the Google Fonts and remote demo photos. Replace those image URLs with your own product photos before launch.

## Customize before publishing
1. **Business identity:** Search for `Gem & Glow`, change the name, colors, copy, and email address (`hello@example.com`) to match your brand.
2. **Products:** Edit the product cards in `index.html` and keep the matching product IDs/prices in `script.js` in sync. Prices are example EGP amounts.
3. **Orders and payment:** Cart and checkout are front-end demos only. They do not take payment or send orders. Connect a backend/payment provider, or change the checkout button to generate a WhatsApp order message and use your business WhatsApp number.
4. **Newsletter:** The form displays a demo confirmation only. Connect an email marketing service/backend if you want to store subscribers.
5. **Contact details and policies:** Add your real email, social links, shipping policy, returns policy, and privacy policy.
6. **Photos:** Use your own product photography or images you have permission to use.
7. **Hosting:** Upload the three site files to a static host such as GitHub Pages, Netlify, or your hosting provider.

## Important
This is a starter storefront, not a production e-commerce checkout. Do not collect payment details in this static site.
